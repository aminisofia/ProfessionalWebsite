about me!

Resume

Hello! I'm Sofie, a video game producer who is currently finishing up her B.S. in Computer Science: Video Game Design at UC: Santa Cruz!

I am extremely passionate about facilitating clear cross-functional communication and creating organized pipelines that make development feel like everything can be completed with ease- despite whatever troubles that may come up. All while creating experiences that feel functionally intuitive, immersive, and artistically inspirational for players. I take pride in my big-picture thinking, strong sense of creative vision, and eagerness to get things done.

On the side, you can find me modifying and reshelling retro handhelds, dabbling in photography, and painting on my iPad while cuddled up with my cats. :)

★current project: stardozing★

Stardozing is a multiplayer "friend-slop" game about cave-diving with your friends. The game has fun physics interactions and features unique customization based on your steam-id.

For this project I handled all the creative direction, production, 2D art, and social media. I'm so proud of my work on stardozing and it's meant so much to me seeing how the art and aesthetics resonate with others.
